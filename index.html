Snapshots of my work

DoorDash 2022–2023

Grocery Substitutions

Customers were getting too many unwanted substitutions due to a confusing new “Substitution Hub” and not knowing how to share preferences. I grew consumer substitutions from an MVP to a loved, full-featured experience—including redesigning the Substitutions Hub and building a scalable preference system. My work cut missing-item complaints 5.7%, captured preferences on 92% of items, and ensured orders were fulfilled perfectly for millions of customers across Grocery, Convenience, Retail, and Alcohol delivery.

DoorDash 2022

Reducing Sign-up Friction for Australian Dashers

To sign up to Dash in Australia, applicants had to complete a complex, 7-step process but many dropped off when asked to provide an Australian Business Number (ABN) to prove their right to work. There was no guidance how to complete this step, and nearly half of applicants were emigrants with potential ESL needs. I identified root causes with the local support team, legal, and operators, then designed in-product education, multilingual support for Spanish and Hindi, provided easy access to create or lookup an existing number, and ABN validation that reduced errors and account deactivations.

Fleetsmith 2019

Custom Apps

Led design and research for a highly technical feature enabling IT Admins to deploy custom applications to their fleet. I partnered closely with specialists and customers to translate technical steps like package management, kernel extensions, and SHA-256 validation into guided flows for non-technical IT admins. This work unblocked $800K ARR in sales contracts and conversations with Enterprise customers.

Fleetsmith 2019–2020

Enterprise App Deployment

As a Series B startup, the early product wasn’t built for scale—yet customers were growing fast and needed to manage thousands of devices in their fleets. I led efforts to evolve our core platform by defining system-level changes with Engineering through mapping our services architecture. We introduced a new primitive, pitched our feature proposals to leadership, and launched 3 features: App Configurations, Optional Global Profile, and Bulk Assignments.

Apple 2021

Apple Business Essentials

After Fleetsmith was acquired by Apple, I defined foundational workflows for its first device management product offering—focusing on app configuration and assignment, collections, and license management. As our team was building new functionality and introducing a visual lift to the existing Business Manager, I collaborated with other Cloud Services teams to ensure consistent use of platform standards and interactions. Announced at WWDC’21. Launched in 2022.

Design values

What I’m about

Principles I lead with—either learned or adopted from smart people.

People first

The heart of everything we do—build with real feedback, needs, and do right by them.

Craft through refinement

Great work is forged by stress testing ideas with regular, quality feedback.

Define clear problems

Setting clear direction and goals are still the most critical part of product development.

Lowest level of detail

Getting to the lowest level of detail in data and feedback reveals how we actually operate—and where the real problems are.

Do what’s fun, not easy

Leverage people’s different strengths and perspectives to craft work we’re proud of—even if it means doing hard things.

We win together

Work is deeply collaborative and great ideas can come from anywhere. Treat each other with an open mind, compassion, and respect.
